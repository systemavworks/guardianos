5. Local VPN Usage

🔒 VPN Usage Statement

GuardianOS Shield uses Android's standard VpnService API exclusively to configure secure DNS servers on the device. The VPN is local and does not redirect traffic to the App owner's servers.

5.1 What the VPN Does

  • Redirects device DNS queries to the CleanBrowsing Adult Filter service (servers 185.228.168.168 and 185.228.169.168), a third-party DNS filtering service.
  • CleanBrowsing blocks adult content domains, malware, phishing, and social networks configured by the guardian on its servers.
  • User HTTP/HTTPS traffic flows directly without passing through any owner servers.

5.2 What the VPN Does NOT Do

  • Does NOT intercept data packets, encrypted communications (HTTPS), or app content.
  • Does NOT log visited URLs, browsing content, or any other usage data.
  • Does NOT send user data to owner servers or undeclared third parties.
  • Does NOT function as a proxy or general traffic tunnel.

5.3 Third-Party DNS Service (CleanBrowsing)

DNS filtering is provided by CleanBrowsing ( cleanbrowsing.org), a company independent of the owner. DNS queries sent to their servers are subject to the CleanBrowsing Privacy Policy. DNS queries are domain names; they do not include communication content or personally identifiable user data.

5.4 Filtering Service Availability

DNS filtering depends on the availability of CleanBrowsing servers. The owner does not guarantee that CleanBrowsing will block 100% of existing inappropriate content, nor that its service will be available uninterruptedly. The App is not a substitute for parental supervision.

6. Privacy and Data Processing

✅ Privacy by Design: GuardianOS Shield does not collect, store, or transmit personal data from the user or minor to any external servers. All processing is performed locally on the device.
  • No user account: The App does not require registration, email, or online profile creation.
  • No analytics: The App does not incorporate analytics SDKs, telemetry, behavior tracking, or advertising.
  • No cloud storage: All configuration, history, and statistics are saved locally on the device via Room Database and DataStore.
  • No sharing data with third parties, except DNS queries to CleanBrowsing described in Section 5.
  • Encrypted parental PIN with EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256-GCM). It is not stored in plain text or sent to any server.

For more information, see our complete Privacy Policy. In case of conflict between these T&C and the Privacy Policy, the Privacy Policy prevails regarding personal data.
